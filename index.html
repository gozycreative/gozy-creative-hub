About

A dependable creative with excellent communication, time management, and problem-solving skills — passionate about delivering outstanding results on every project.

From the vibrant city of Abuja, Nigeria, Aleke Chigozie has built a career transforming ideas into compelling visual stories. As an Artisan, Video Editor, and Graphic Designer, his journey began with a passion for craftsmanship and creativity — a passion that grew into technical mastery across video editing, motion graphics, and brand design.

At Gozy Creative Hub, he has spent years editing professional videos for social media, corporate organizations, churches, and promotional campaigns, applying colour correction, audio enhancement, and motion graphics to bring every project to life — alongside designing flyers, posters, banners, and logos that help brands communicate clearly and confidently.

His path as an Artisan taught him precision and reliability — skills he carries into every creative project, from concept to delivery. Beyond the studio, he finds inspiration in photography, reading, travel, and learning new technologies.
