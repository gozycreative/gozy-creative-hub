Professional Profile

Aleke Chigozie is a creative, results-oriented video editor, graphic designer, and entrepreneur based in Abuja, Nigeria, operating under his brand, Gozie Creative Hub. Driven by a deep passion for visual storytelling and digital strategy, Chigozie transforms complex ideas into compelling brand identities, promotional campaigns, and high-impact visual media.

Foundation & Mentorship

His foundational creative journey was inspired by his family—his bedrock of strength, led by his beloved mother ('My Superwoman')—and mentored by Miss Teni ('Queen of Graphics'), who taught him graphic design principles and fueled his passion for visual excellence.

Brand Leadership & Execution

Continuously expanding his professional capabilities, Chigozie completed the Youthrive Entrepreneurship Program in July 2024, solidifying his skills in business execution, leadership, and digital innovation. As a skilled artisan and technical creative, he specializes in video editing, motion graphics, photo editing, and brand identity design.

Technical Skillset

Software & Tools: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe After Effects, CapCut, Canva, Botpress AI, HTML/CSS.
