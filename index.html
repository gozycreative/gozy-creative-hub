About My Life & Journey

Biography, Mentorship & Brand Foundation

Professional Profile

Aleke Chigozie is a creative, results-oriented video editor, graphic designer, and entrepreneur based in Abuja, Nigeria, operating under his brand, Gozie Creative Hub. Driven by a deep passion for visual storytelling and digital strategy, Chigozie transforms complex ideas into compelling brand identities, promotional campaigns, and high-impact visual media.

Foundation & Mentorship

His foundational creative journey was inspired by his family—his bedrock of strength, led by his beloved mother—and mentored by Miss Teni ('Queen of Graphics'), who taught him graphic design principles and fueled his passion for visual excellence.

Family & Personal Bedrock

Grounded in faith, perseverance, and strong personal relationships, Chigozie draws ongoing inspiration from his family background, which fuels his dedication to building long-term value through creative business solutions.

Technical Skillset
